About Angelov Realty Group

Angelov Realty Group was founded on a simple idea: real estate should feel transparent, calm, and strategic, not confusing or rushed. We believe every buyer and seller deserves clear information and a partner who listens before offering advice.

Meet Ivaylo Angelov

As a full-time licensed real estate professional, Ivaylo blends market knowledge with a calm, educator mindset. Clients appreciate his ability to break down complex contracts, negotiate thoughtfully, and keep communication consistent from the first tour to closing day.

Before real estate, Ivaylo built his career in customer-facing roles that demanded attention to detail, strong ethics, and long-term relationships. That background now informs how Angelov Realty Group protects each client’s interests with integrity.

Our values in every transaction

Honesty first

We tell you what we would want to hear if we were in your position, even when that means slowing down or rethinking an approach.

Clarity over pressure

Our goal is to make sure you understand your options and feel empowered to decide what works best for you and your timeline.

Relationship focused

We stay connected even after closing, serving as a resource for questions about home ownership, local service providers, and future moves.
