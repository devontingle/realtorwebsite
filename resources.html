Resources & guides

Real estate decisions feel easier when you understand the moving parts. These quick guides are designed to give you a plain-language overview of common questions buyers and sellers ask in today’s market.

Understanding closing costs

Closing costs include lender fees, title charges, property taxes, and prepaid items like homeowner’s insurance. We walk you through estimated numbers early so there are no surprises when you receive your closing disclosure.

Inspection basics for buyers

A home inspection is your opportunity to learn how the home is performing. We help you prioritize repairs, negotiate where appropriate, and focus on safety, structure, and systems that matter most.

Preparing your home for photos

Online photos are the first showing most buyers see. Simple steps like decluttering surfaces, opening blinds, and adding neutral touches can help your home look brighter, larger, and more inviting.
